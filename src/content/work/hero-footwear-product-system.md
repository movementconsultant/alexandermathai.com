HERO Footwear treats product, brand, and commerce as one build rather than three separate workstreams — biomechanics-informed design choices, a consistent brand voice, and the ecommerce and fulfillment-partner infrastructure needed to actually ship.

No clinical, injury-prevention, or medical performance claims are made for any HERO product. Biomechanics-informed means the design process is informed by movement science thinking, not that any product is validated as a medical device or treatment.
