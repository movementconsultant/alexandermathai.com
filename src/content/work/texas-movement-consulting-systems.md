The consulting practice exists to answer one question for each client: what is actually constraining execution here, and what is the smallest owned system that removes it? That has meant AI workflow design, operating-system documentation, brand-architecture work, and embedded advisory, depending on what the diagnosis surfaces.

This is reported by the founder as a historical total across the practice's full history, not a claim about any single current engagement, active roster, or specific client outcome — no client names or case-level results are published here without confirmed permission.
